02 / THE RESEARCH AGENT

A pipeline that knows when to ask for help.

The agent normalizes seeded evidence, checks every source, runs contradiction rules, and routes uncertain claims to a human review queue.

01

Seed

Start from all 100 requested apps and first-party developer entry points.

02

Fetch

Check evidence URLs with timeouts, retries, and guarded-page detection.

03

Normalize

Map auth, access, surface, MCP, buildability, and confidence.

04

Challenge

Flag contradictions such as “Ready” paired with a gated production path.

05

Review

Send low-confidence and negative-evidence claims to a person.
